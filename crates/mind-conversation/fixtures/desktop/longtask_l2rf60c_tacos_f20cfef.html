Tacos

Back to all dinners

Ingredients

  • 500 g ground beef
  • 8 corn tortillas
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp taco spice
  • 1 cup shredded cheese
  • 1 lime

Steps

  1. Brown the beef with the onion.
  2. Stir in the taco spice and a splash of water.
  3. Warm the tortillas in a dry pan.
  4. Fill, top with cheese, squeeze lime over.
