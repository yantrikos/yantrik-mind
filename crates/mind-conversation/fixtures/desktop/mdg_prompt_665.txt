Dimensions: ENTITY (typed: PERSON, SOFTWARE, MODEL, DEVICE, CONCEPT...), REL (USES, PREFERS, DEPENDS_ON, PART_OF, CAUSES, CONTRADICTS...), STATE with validity (valid_from / valid_until), EVENT (actor, from, to, time), ACTION, GOAL (objective + constraints), CONSTRAINT (e.g. VRAM<24GB), QUANTITY with units, TIME, CAUSE. Every claim can carry: mode (OBSERVED, REMEMBERED, INFERRED, PREDICTED, HYPOTHESIZED, DESIRED), conf (0..1), src (USER, DOCUMENT, TOOL, MEMORY, INFERENCE...). Conflicts are explicit, and validity intervals turn a changed preference into a non-contradiction. Repeated structure can be factored (COMMON blocks). Numbers are native typed values, not words.
